Software as an extension of infrastructure and security engineering.

Many of these projects began with an operational problem: too much data, fragmented evidence, repetitive troubleshooting, limited visibility or a need to understand relationships across multiple systems. The goal is to turn that problem into a repeatable engineering workflow.
